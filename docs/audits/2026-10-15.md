The security policy now documents the verified private reporting channel in
English and PT-PT.

## Controlled follow-up in this change

Issue #5 records a portability defect: fixed check subprocesses inherited their
stream encoding and the parent decoded output with its locale. A real Python
test printing Portuguese stdout and stderr failed when `PYTHONIOENCODING=ascii`
was inherited. The fix selects UTF-8 in the child environment and captured
output without changing the parent environment or the fixed command allowlist.

The regression failed before the fix and passed afterward. The candidate suite
contains 46 tests. Its full cross-platform CI must pass before merge; the PR's
checks are authoritative. The roadmap now reflects native OpenAI and Anthropic
adapters already present in source. New audit and maintenance documentation is
available in both English and PT-PT.
