# Code of Conduct

We strive for an inclusive, constructive technical community. Be respectful, discuss behavior and evidence rather than personal characteristics, and do not tolerate harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing or intentional disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may remove disruptive content and restrict participation to protect contributors and users. To report a conduct issue privately, use GitHub's private contact mechanism once configured; if unavailable, open an issue requesting a confidential contact method without sharing sensitive details.

All participants should approach security research and engineering disagreements in good faith. This policy does not supersede applicable laws or GitHub's platform rules.
